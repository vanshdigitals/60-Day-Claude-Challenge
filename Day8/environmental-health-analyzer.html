Methodology & data notes. This is a demonstration app running on a simulated demo dataset for 17–22 Aug 2026: deterministic values seeded from a real 15 Aug 2026 baseline (monsoon season). Dates on or after today (17 Aug 2026) are not live or verified observations. AQI figures follow the US EPA AQI scale; PM2.5 is derived from AQI using EPA breakpoints; PM10 is estimated from PM2.5 and is indicative only. Water-quality scores are modeled from published TDS, hardness and BIS-compliance reports (not a live feed) and reflect typical municipal-plus-groundwater conditions, which vary by locality. AQI is dynamic and updates hourly — treat this as a point-in-time reading, not a forecast. Not medical advice.
