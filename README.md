# 60-Day Claude Challenge

## Introduction

I'm a student exploring the intersection of graphic design, AI-assisted coding, AI agents, research, and content creation. This repository documents a 60-day personal challenge where I use Claude daily to learn, build, and grow — primarily as a graphic designer, with AI and building skills as supporting strengths.

This is a public learning journey, not a portfolio of finished expertise. Expect visible progress, mistakes, and iteration.

## About the Challenge

Each day, I complete an assigned challenge, document what I learned or built, save evidence (notes, files, or screenshots), and make a Git commit. The challenge is self-directed and adapts as I learn what I need most — some days lean into design practice, others into AI tools, coding, or research.

Day 1 starts with understanding my own AI working style and personality — how I use AI, my strengths and gaps, and how I make decisions — so the following 59 days build on an honest starting point rather than guesswork.

## Goals for 60 Days

- Build a consistent daily practice of learning and shipping in public
- Strengthen my core graphic design skills: visual systems, layout, typography, color, and design thinking
- Get hands-on with AI-assisted coding, AI agents, and research as supporting tools
- Produce real, documented evidence of growth — not just notes, but things I actually made
- Build toward a graphic design internship or entry-level role by the end of the challenge

## Primary Focus: Graphic Design

Graphic design is the core goal of this challenge. Most days will involve deliberate design practice — studying references, building design systems, working on layout and typography, and producing real design output (not just AI-generated visuals, but work I've directed, refined, and can explain).

## Secondary Interests

Supporting the primary goal, I'll also be exploring:

- **AI tools** — using AI as a research and design assistant
- **AI agents** — understanding agent architecture and workflows at a beginner-to-intermediate level
- **Vibe coding** — AI-assisted building of small websites, prototypes, and tools
- **Research** — investigating design trends, AI developments, and technical concepts relevant to my work
- **Content creation** — documenting and sharing what I learn, primarily as a byproduct of this challenge rather than a separate goal
